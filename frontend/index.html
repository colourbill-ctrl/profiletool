ICC Profile Tool — inspect, view & edit ICC profiles in your browser

A free, browser-based ICC profile viewer, inspector and editor. Open any .icc or .icm profile to browse its 128-byte header and full tag directory, view tone-response curves, chromaticity, CLUT and gamut visualizations, evaluate device-to-PCS transforms, and round-trip edit the profile as XML or JSON. Everything runs locally with a WebAssembly build of the ICC's IccProfLib — your profiles are never uploaded.
